BOTTOM LINE
411 residents visited one of 4 urban wilds and proposed changes. Litter and dumping cleanup was the most-requested change (62% of designs), followed by signage and wayfinding (50%) and trail repair (48%). Allandale Woods had the highest share asking to leave it wild (49%).

KEY FINDINGS
1. Chandler Pond (155 visits): top request was litter and dumping cleanup (63%).
2. Allandale Woods (123 visits): top request was litter and dumping cleanup (57%).
3. Roslindale Wetlands (72 visits): top request was trail repair (64%).
4. Sherrin Woods (61 visits): top request was litter and dumping cleanup (72%).
5. Most common answer to "who is this space for": neighbors out for a daily walk (28%).

ABOUT THIS DATA
Residents were asked to: Pick an urban wild near you (the app shows the closest three); Visit it and take 3 photos: the entrance, the best spot, the worst spot; In the builder, place up to 5 changes on a simple map: trail, signage, seating, lighting, cleanup, accessible entrance, or leave it wild; Write one sentence on who the space is for.
Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 64% of responses and residents 65+ are 8%; Allston residents are 41%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.
Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
