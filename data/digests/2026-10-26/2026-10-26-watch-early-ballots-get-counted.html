Resident Input BriefSunday, November 1, 2026
TOSabino Piemonte, Election Department
FROMTea Party resident input program
REAdvance processing of early voted ballots for the Nov 3, 2026 State Election, City Hall, Room 801, Oct 26 through Nov 3, mostly 8:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m.; Election Day from 8 a.m. until complete (agenda)
BOTTOM LINE
271 residents took part. Clarity and transparency averaged 3.6 out of 5: 54% gave a 4 or 5 and 17% gave a 1 or 2. The most common theme in their notes was "Hard to see or hear the process" (35%), followed by "Process was explained well" (28%).
KEY FINDINGS
  1. 54% rated clarity and transparency 4 or 5; 17% rated it 1 or 2.
  2. Top themes: Hard to see or hear the process (35%); Process was explained well (28%); Materials not posted in advance (21%).
  3. Residents 65+ gave the lowest average (3.3/5, n = 21).

ABOUT THIS DATA

Residents were asked to: Check the processing schedule in the app; days and times vary; Go to City Hall, Room 801, and observe for at least 30 minutes. Don't photograph ballots; Record the steps you saw, such as envelope checks, ballot removal, and tabulation, and where observers were placed; Answer two questions: could you see clearly, and did anyone explain what was happening?; Write 2–3 sentences on how confident the process made you feel.

Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 67% of responses and residents 65+ are 8%; Allston residents are 43%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.

Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
