BOTTOM LINE
498 residents took part. Overall experience averaged 3.5 out of 5: 50% gave a 4 or 5 and 18% gave a 1 or 2. The most common theme in their notes was "Hard to find; signage missing" (35%), followed by "Accessibility barriers" (27%).

KEY FINDINGS
1. 50% rated overall experience 4 or 5; 18% rated it 1 or 2.
2. Top themes: Hard to find; signage missing (35%); Accessibility barriers (27%); Spaces closed or locked (21%).
3. Residents 45–64 gave the lowest average (3.4/5, n = 77).

ABOUT THIS DATA
Residents were asked to: Choose a recent large development in your neighborhood; Look for public benefits a passerby can check: open space or a plaza, public seating, ground-floor community space, bike parking, sidewalk improvements, or signs saying a space is open to the public; Mark each one as present and open, present but closed or locked, or missing or can't tell; Take at least 2 photos of what you found; Write one sentence: did the neighborhood get something from this building?.
Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 60% of responses and residents 65+ are 7%; Allston residents are 40%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.
Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
