REReparations Task Force virtual public meeting, Thu, Oct 29, 2026, 5:30 p.m.: updates on the Tufts research report, researcher presentations, then public comment of up to two minutes per person (agenda)
BOTTOM LINE
175 residents took part. Clarity and transparency averaged 2.9 out of 5: 28% gave a 4 or 5 and 35% gave a 1 or 2. The most common theme in their notes was "Hard to see or hear the process" (33%), followed by "Wanted more time for public comment" (26%).
KEY FINDINGS
  1. 28% rated clarity and transparency 4 or 5; 35% rated it 1 or 2.
  2. Top themes: Hard to see or hear the process (33%); Wanted more time for public comment (26%); Materials not posted in advance (23%).
  3. Residents 35–44 gave the lowest average (2.7/5, n = 29).

ABOUT THIS DATA

Residents were asked to: By Wed, Oct 28, submit one question you want the Task Force or researchers to answer; Register for and join the virtual meeting on Oct 29 at 5:30 p.m; Note the main points from the Tufts research update and researcher presentations; Record the questions raised during public comment and how they were answered; Optional: give up to two minutes of public comment yourself and log what you said; Add your own take in 2–3 sentences.

Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 61% of responses and residents 65+ are 8%; Allston residents are 38%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.

Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
