ABOUT THIS DATA

Residents were asked to: Before the meeting, pick one docket from the agenda packet. The app pulls the packet once it's posted; Record what happened to it: passed, referred to committee, amended, or tabled; Note who spoke and the main argument on each side; Add your own take in 2–3 sentences.

Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 57% of responses and residents 65+ are 8%; Allston residents are 44%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.

Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
