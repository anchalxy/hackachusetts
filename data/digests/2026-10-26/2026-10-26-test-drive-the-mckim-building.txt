BOTTOM LINE
263 residents took part. Overall experience averaged 3.1 out of 5: 37% gave a 4 or 5 and 30% gave a 1 or 2. The most common theme in their notes was "Hard to find; signage missing" (36%), followed by "Accessibility barriers" (24%).

KEY FINDINGS
1. 37% rated overall experience 4 or 5; 30% rated it 1 or 2.
2. Top themes: Hard to find; signage missing (36%); Accessibility barriers (24%); Spaces closed or locked (24%).
3. Residents 35–44 gave the lowest average (3.1/5, n = 37).

ABOUT THIS DATA
Residents were asked to: Enter the Central Library and use the in-app timer to see how long it takes to reach the McKim Building; Find three things: the Commonwealth Salon, a place to sit and study, and a desk or staff member who can explain how to use the research collections; Rate four things from 1 to 5: wayfinding, accessibility, study space, and building condition; Take at least 2 photos (one of the best spot, one of the worst); Answer one question: before today, had you heard of the Bridge to McKim program?; Write one sentence on what the renovation should fix first.
Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 67% of responses and residents 65+ are 7%; Allston residents are 39%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.
Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
