BOTTOM LINE
388 residents took part. Support for the proposal averaged 3.1 out of 5: 29% gave a 4 or 5 and 24% gave a 1 or 2. The most common theme in their notes was "Evening and weekend hours" (35%), followed by "Free or low-cost programming" (29%).
KEY FINDINGS
  1. 29% rated support for the proposal 4 or 5; 24% rated it 1 or 2.
  2. Top themes: Evening and weekend hours (35%); Free or low-cost programming (29%); Youth programs (22%).
  3. Residents 65+ gave the lowest average (3.0/5, n = 21).

ABOUT THIS DATA

Residents were asked to: Walk or bike to 290 North Beacon in Brighton; Take 2 photos: the front entrance and the street around it; In the builder, fill a weekly calendar with uses such as live music, rehearsal space, recording, gallery, classes, open studio, community meetings or youth programs; Mark which slot should be the pilot: the one thing to try first; Write one sentence on who the space is for.

Opt-in sample, not a random survey. Residents 18–34 are 64% of responses and residents 65+ are 5%; Brighton residents are 37%. Figures describe participants, not all residents.

Aggregated and pseudonymized. No names, emails, addresses or individual identities are included. Quotes are attributed by neighborhood, and by age group only when that group has 10 or more participants.
