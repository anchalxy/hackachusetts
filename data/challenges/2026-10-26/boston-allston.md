**The task**

Users pick a large recent development near them; the app suggests nearby projects. Then they check what a passerby can actually see and use.

1. Choose a recent large development in your neighborhood.
2. Look for public benefits a passerby can check: open space or a plaza, public seating, ground-floor community space, bike parking, sidewalk improvements, or signs saying a space is open to the public.

**Verification:** The app checks that photo locations match the chosen project. The AI checks that the photos match each rating.

**What the POC receives:** A list of buildings checked, with the share of visible benefits found open, closed or missing. It includes a photo map of locked or missing amenities, broken down by neighborhood and age.
